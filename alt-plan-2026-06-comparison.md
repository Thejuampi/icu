## Decision Rules

- If HRV is suppressed, resting HR is elevated, or life stress is clearly high, replace the Tuesday intensity session with `75-90m` of Z2.
- On cold days, use power and RPE as the primary anchors. Heart rate is a guardrail, not the sole steering wheel.
- If drift is already above roughly `6%` before the midpoint despite normal fueling, cap the final steady block or shorten the ride by `20-30m`.
- Keep hard days at two per week maximum. More load should come from aerobic time and durable steady work.
